infoAbout Musify

Musify is a personal open-source project, created and run by one developer.

What is Musify?

Musify is a free Android music player that lets you search for songs, stream them, build playlists, read lyrics and download tracks for offline listening. It is written with Flutter and follows Material You design guidelines, so it adapts to your device's colors and looks at home on modern Android.

Our mission

We believe a music app should be simple, respectful and transparent. Musify has no advertisements, no paywalls and does not require an account. All of the application's source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can inspect, audit, improve or fork it.

Who builds it?

Musify is a personal project. It is not a company or a commercial service: it was created and is maintained by Valeri Gokadze, an independent developer, with help from contributors and translators around the world. Contributions of any kind, such as code, translations, bug reports and ideas, are welcome on GitHub.

What this website is for

This website is the home of my personal project. It explains the features, links to the official downloads, shows the latest release notes and answers common questions. Musify's official sources are the GitHub repository and F-Droid.

Supporting the project

Musify is developed in spare time and kept free for everyone. If you enjoy it, you can star the repository, share it with friends, contribute, or donate through Ko-fi.

Content disclaimer

Musify does not host any music files on this website or on its own servers. The app is a client that accesses publicly available content. Users are responsible for complying with the laws and terms of service that apply in their country.
