Bug reports and feature requests

The fastest way to get help is to open an issue on our GitHub issue tracker. Please include your Android version, the Musify version and clear steps to reproduce the problem.

Questions and discussions

For general questions, ideas and help from the community, use GitHub Discussions. Many answers are collected in the community FAQ.

Support the project

You can support development through Ko-fi.

Website, privacy or copyright questions

For anything related to this website, its privacy policy or a copyright concern, open an issue titled "Website" on GitHub or message the maintainer through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
