Musify is a free, open-source, ad-free music app for Android that offers streaming and offline listening.

Built around a clean Material You interface. Search for songs, build playlists, read lyrics and save tracks for offline playback, with no advertisements and no account required.

help Frequently Asked Questions

Is Musify really free?

Yes. Musify is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertisements inside the app. Development is supported by voluntary donations.

Which platforms are supported?

Musify is built for Android. Download the APK from GitHub or install the F-Droid build from the F-Droid store.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Download any track from within the app and it stays on your device so you can play it without an internet connection.

Does Musify collect my personal data?

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising trackers. Read our Privacy Policy for details about both the app and this website.

Where can I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub or visit the contact page.

Is this website the only official source?

Musify's official sources are the GitHub repository and F-Droid. Be careful with copies of the app from other sites.
