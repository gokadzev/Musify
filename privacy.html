Overview

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the Musify website and how it relates to the Musify Android app. We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

Information we collect on this website

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, e-mail addresses or other personal details on this site. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services listed below may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and time of visit.

Cookies and advertising

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

Third-party services

  • GitHub: used to load the latest release information, release notes, screenshots and the app logo.
  • jsDelivr: serves the style and script libraries used by this site.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typefaces used by this site.
  • Shields.io: renders the project statistics badges.
  • Google AdSense: displays advertising, as described above.

These services receive your IP address when your browser requests their files and are governed by their own privacy policies.

The Musify Android app

The app does not require an account and contains no advertising. Playlists, favorites, settings and downloaded songs are stored on your device. The app contacts online music sources to search for and stream content, and those services may receive standard network request data. The app's source code is public so you can verify its behavior.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not collect personal data directly, most requests should be directed to the third-party providers above. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please see our contact page.
